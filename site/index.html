Grave Truth Kiroween 2026 · Skeleton Crew · local only

A figure stays only when the document states it.

Grave Truth checks one synthetic explanation of benefits, or one synthetic lease. It quotes the sentence, or it says not found.

  1. Input. The patient responsibility is $42.15.
  2. Check. That sentence is kept only when the synthetic bill states it. A penalty the bill never mentions is not found.
  3. Result. A dispute letter or a clause list you can download. Each cited line is linked to its span. Anything else says not found.

Research demo on synthetic text. Not a medical device and not legal advice. Nothing is sent off this machine. Do not paste a real bill, a real lease, or anyone's personal information.
