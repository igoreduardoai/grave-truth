# Product

Grave Truth is a citation-grounded document-agent skeleton for Kiroween 2026, Skeleton Crew. One library answers from source spans. Two applications sit on top of it: a synthetic explanation-of-benefits explainer and a synthetic lease clause checker.

People using the demos are reading a fixture, not their own bill or lease. The product shows when a sentence in the file supports a claim and when it does not.

## Limits

- Research demo. It is not a medical device and not legal advice.
- It does not decide coverage, coding, or whether a lease clause is enforceable.
- It quotes text. It does not know whether that text is true in the world.
- Fixtures are synthetic. Do not paste real patient or tenant data.
