# Fixture safety

Documents under `fixtures/` are invented. Names like Riverside Clinic and 200 Harbor Street are not real accounts.

Do not add real member ids, addresses, medical record numbers, or scans of bills. Do not add a sentence whose only job is to smuggle a number the rest of the file does not mean.

When two clauses share a dollar amount, keep both wordings distinct so a question can cite one subject.
